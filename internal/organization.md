# MAPLE Organizational Structure

MAPLE is an OpenRockets initiative. Its work is organized through the Board of Directors and the following departments.

Arjun serves as MAPLE’s Executive Director, guiding the project and supporting its departments.

## Board of Directors

The Board of Directors includes the directors who lead MAPLE’s departments. Each director leads their department and participates in project decisions.

## Departments

| Department | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Executive** | Leads MAPLE’s overall work, guides its direction and coordinates the departments. |
| **Research** | Finds and reviews sources and researches topics relevant to MAPLE and its white paper. |
| **Writing and Documentation** | Writes and edits the white paper in Overleaf and develops MAPLE’s supporting documents. |
| **Communications** | Coordinates volunteers and communication among the team and department directors. |
| **Social Media** | Creates and shares updates through Substack, X and other relevant channels. |
| **Outreach** | Connects with professionals, organizations, potential partners and other external contacts. |
| **Legal and Licensing** | Helps develop the license and coordinates legal feedback. |
| **Technology** | Assists with the website, repositories and other technical work as needed. |
